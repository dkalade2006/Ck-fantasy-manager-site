Product capabilities

CK Fantasy Manager is designed to complement Yahoo Fantasy—not replace league administration or lineup submission. Capabilities below distinguish prototype work from future directions.

01 / PROTOTYPE

Roster intelligence

Roster views, player comparison and local lineup decision support. Interface refinements are still being validated.

02 / PROTOTYPE

Personal front office

Configurable presentation, appearance preferences, football-inspired palettes and personal team branding.

03 / PRODUCT DIRECTION

Performance analytics

Historical scoring, lineup efficiency and transparent retrospective analysis remain areas of ongoing development.

04 / PRODUCT DIRECTION

Advanced intelligence

Transaction evaluation, roster risk, contextual reports and scenario analysis are roadmap directions, not public-release commitments.

Yahoo Fantasy integration

Authorization-gated track

Separate from the sprint sequence

Live Yahoo Fantasy Sports API integration remains subject to provider approval and applicable permissions. Prototype and synthetic/demo capabilities should not be interpreted as authorized access to live league data.

The intended integration is read-only: CK Fantasy Manager will not submit roster changes, trades or waiver claims. No integration launch date is announced.

About the project

CK Fantasy Manager is an educational systems-design and personal software-development project. Its initial scope is one authorized fantasy football manager in one Yahoo Fantasy league, for private, noncommercial use.

The application is in private prototype development. This public website is informational only; it does not provide account access or an application login.

Privacy & data use

This informational page has no account registration, analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or application-level collection of personal information. The hosting provider may process standard request information under its own policies.

Any live integration will use authorized access only, with minimum necessary permissions. Provider data will be processed and retained only as permitted by the applicable agreement. Historical storage, data exports and external AI processing will remain disabled unless authorized. Credentials and tokens will not be published or shared.

No sale or redistribution of fantasy league data is planned. This notice will be updated before any live application is released.
